# VC152 / 1.5.9 – progressive levels
Safe patch from VC151. 300 levels: deterministic count curve 24–300, 7 progression bands; all fruit counts even; attack-free stages 1–20, first introductions 21–26, scheduled attacks limited to 2–7 per level, rotating 1–4 types, postponed during active attacks, persistent web or defense cinematics. Defense hints and first-win five-stage reward targeted to upcoming attacks; saves, shop and inventory schema preserved. Staggered solid obstacle rows and optional vertical divider; static rocks, moving breakable wooden crates, existing cages and ice preserved. Target 0–8 solid barriers on tall screens; short screens may use fewer for clearance. Standalone opening/closing gates and new horizontal divider variants are not included. `tools/levels_vc152.csv` lists every planned stage. Tests initialize 300 levels on 412x915 and 360x600, validate counts, pairs, obstacle spacing and schedule, plus selected staged defense tests. These tests are not proof of full playthrough solvability or on-device performance. No online play/audio changes.
