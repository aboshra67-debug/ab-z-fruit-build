# V1.2.3 / VC116 — difficulty and visual balance
Safe patch from supplied V1.2.2 / VC115. Original native package, `zfruit_v1` save key, 300 stages, 24–500 fruits, four base channel slots, power-ups, and channel-only match rules retained. No native Java changes or GitHub writes.
- Revised radius progression: larger early fruits (level 10 logical radius 35→41 on 412×915, 28→33 on 360×640); advanced stage radius unchanged at level 117. Early type count at level 10 is 11 vs 14; late stages retain up to 28 types. Longer nested pair groups in advanced stages, with logical stack depth still <=3.
- Reserve field follows the funnel profile and obstacle clearance envelope rather than stopping at one global obstacle line. Seeded denser scatter; level 117 has 392 total fruits and initially 40 visible vs 30 at 412×915, 48 vs 30 at 360×640. Hidden fruit don't render clipped under the HUD and enter with short fade. Reserve descends faster when the falling path is clear. Experimental simultaneous descent during falling was reverted after detecting trapping on slopes; clearance widened around terrain and obstacles.
- Crates rotate faster and through a slightly larger angular range; retains actual impact/breakage, rocks, bees, birds, cages, bombs and ice. No new obstacle types. Offline scene JPEG saturation reduced 15% and contrast 5%; no runtime filter. Native assets, save compatibility, APK signature and ZIP alignment checked.
- APK repacked from supplied VC115 with new web assets and version fields; signed using existing shared test certificate. Not fresh Gradle or actual Android test. Automated logical sequence checks and controlled Matter.js solver paths do not prove every human move or every stage's full physical solvability. The comparison with the original is based on its provided level 117 recording, not a reverse engineered difficulty model.
- Final verification: all 300 exact counts, parity, hidden-input and logical stack sequences at both screen sizes. All 14 controlled full physics runs (1, 5, 10, 25, 80, 117, 300 × two sizes) won, including level 300. Both overflow and action test suites passed. Chromium preview of level 117 had no page errors. Logs in `tools/results/vc116_*.log`.
