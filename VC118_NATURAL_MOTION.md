# V1.2.5 / VC118 — Natural movement Safe Patch
Baseline: VC117. Same native package, web assets, levels.js difficulty curve, count of 300 stages, four-slot base channel, save schema and signing certificate. No GitHub writes.

- Replaced collective reserve shift with individual downward swept-circle clearance, bounded speed and acceleration, spatial hash, and local active-fall clearance. A blocked column does not force unrelated columns to stop. No artificial jumps or overlap for reserve movement.
- Removed foreground collision bypass, manual separation, upward impulses, artificial release velocities and teleport recovery. A released fruit can wait on a suspended fruit until its support is released or it rolls naturally around it. Zero restitution on fruit and obstacles; native Matter contact solver retains small polygon/contact tolerance.
- Increased clearance above the slopes and around obstacles for an entire falling fruit to fit. This necessarily changes the initial distribution/visible density (e.g. level 10 tall 26 -> 22). Fruit size and total counts unchanged. The display clips incoming fruits smoothly at the HUD boundary without fading/scaling them into view.
- Rotating crates pause near a falling fruit to avoid artificial kicks. Bombs still release nearby fruit and show effects but no longer apply a launch impulse. Birds hold fruit in its existing slot and release it from there instead of transporting it across the field. Queued taps retry only when deep obstruction has cleared.
- The APK was repackaged from signed VC117 with updated web assets and version metadata; no fresh Gradle build or physical Android test. V2 signature, original certificate, assets, CRC and alignment verified. Native DEX/resources unchanged. Existing test key retained; production signing is a separate future task.
- Controlled Node tests: support release, independently blocked reserve, queued tap, ice/crate/bird/bomb, channel overflow, channel-only matching, all 300 level initializations and logical sequence checks at two sizes. See tools/results/vc118_* logs. These are engine simulations, not proof of all human paths or a guarantee of zero numerical contact overlap.
- Full controlled physics paths: 1, 5, 10, 25, 80 won on both sizes in the intermediate pass before the final contact-release tolerance adjustment; 117 and 300 won on both sizes with the final game.js. The final contact and regression checks passed on both sizes. Level 117 simulation time 615s tall / 638s short, level 300 802s / 818s; these are simulated game time, not wall-clock human play times. The honest tradeoff is slower physical completion than the previous collision-bypass patch.
